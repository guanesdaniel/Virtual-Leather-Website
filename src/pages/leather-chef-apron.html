Why leather in the kitchen

  • Real leather that gets better with age
  • Your name, initials or logo engraved, not printed
  • Black, tan or dark brown, with straps and pocket in a second colour
  • Cut to the wearer's height and build, with adjustable straps

Looking after it

Wipe the apron with a damp cloth and let it dry naturally, away from direct heat. Every few months, feed the leather with a conditioner such as our leather care grease to keep it supple.
