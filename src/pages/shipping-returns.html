Shipping, returns & refunds

Last updated: {{site.policiesUpdated}}

This policy applies to orders placed on virtualleather.net. Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights as a consumer.

Made to order

Our aprons and most of our leather goods are made by hand to order and personalised for you. After you order, we send a design proof to the email or phone number you gave us. We start production once you approve it.

Production and delivery times

We make your order in about 3 days after you approve the design proof. We then ship it from Colombia and send you a tracking number by email or WhatsApp. How long delivery takes depends on where you are and on customs in your country.

Shipping costs

Shipping is included in the price of our aprons, wherever we send them. For other items, any shipping cost is shown at checkout before you pay.

Customs, duties and import taxes

Your order is shipped from Colombia. Unless we've agreed otherwise, you pay any import duties, taxes and customs fees charged in your country. The carrier may ask you for these before delivery.

Changing or cancelling an order

If you need to change your design, tell us before you approve the proof. You can cancel your order for a full refund at any time until we engrave your approved design on the leather. Once it's engraved, the item is personalised to you, so we can't cancel it.

Returns

Personalised and made-to-measure items

Personalised and made-to-measure items are made to your specification. Consumer cancellation rights do not apply to them. This is the case under the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 and the EU Consumer Rights Directive. So we can't accept returns of personalised items if you change your mind. This does not affect your rights if an item is faulty or not as described (see below).

Items that are not personalised

If you are a consumer, you can cancel an order for an item that is not personalised (for example our leather care grease) within 14 days of receiving it, without giving a reason. Tell us by email at {{site.email}} and return the item unused within 14 days of telling us. Unless the item was faulty, you pay the return postage. We refund the price and the original standard delivery cost within 14 days of receiving the item back.

Faulty, damaged or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged or faulty, or doesn't match the proof you approved, contact us as soon as possible. Include your order number and photos. We'll repair it, remake it or refund you, as appropriate and as your consumer rights require.

Leather is a natural material. Small variations in tone, grain and markings are normal and are not a fault.

How refunds are paid

We refund to the original payment method. Your bank or card provider may take a few days to show the refund.
