Returns

Personalised and made-to-measure items

Personalised and made-to-measure items are made to your specification. Consumer cancellation rights do not apply to them. This is the case under the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 and the EU Consumer Rights Directive. So we can't accept returns of personalised items if you change your mind. This does not affect your rights if an item is faulty or not as described (see below).

Items that are not personalised

If you are a consumer, you can cancel an order for an item that is not personalised (for example our leather care grease) within 14 days of receiving it, without giving a reason. Tell us by email at {{site.email}} and return the item unused within 14 days of telling us. Unless the item was faulty, you pay the return postage. We refund the price and the original standard delivery cost within 14 days of receiving the item back.

Faulty, damaged or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged or faulty, or doesn't match the proof you approved, contact us as soon as possible. Include your order number and photos. We'll repair it, remake it or refund you, as appropriate and as your consumer rights require.

Leather is a natural material. Small variations in tone, grain and markings are normal and are not a fault.

How refunds are paid

We refund to the original payment method. Your bank or card provider may take a few days to show the refund.
