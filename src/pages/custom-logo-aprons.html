How a business order works

  1. Send us your logo and how many aprons you need, with each person's height and build (and name, if you want names).
  2. We send you a quote. Prices for business and multi-apron orders are agreed directly with us. A single apron is $185 with shipping included.
  3. You approve the design proof. Nothing is made until you're happy with it.
  4. We make the aprons by hand and ship them with a tracking number. We'll confirm the timing with your quote.
