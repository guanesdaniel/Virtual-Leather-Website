Good to know

Questions, answered.

How do you size the apron?

Every apron is made to measure. Tell us the wearer's height and weight when you order and we cut the apron to fit. The neck and waist straps are also adjustable.

Will I see my design before you make it?

Yes. Before production we send you a design proof by email or WhatsApp. We only start once you approve it.

What can you engrave, and where?

Names, short phrases, dates, monograms and logos. There are six engraving positions, plus the pocket on the BBQ apron (two on the split-leg and woodworking aprons), shown as numbers in the designer preview. {{#if extraEngraving}}Two engravings are included in the price, and the chest (spots 1 and 2) counts as one. Each extra spot is $5.{{else}}Personalisation is included in the price.{{/if extraEngraving}} Send logos as PNG, JPG, PDF or SVG.

Which leather colours can I choose?

Black, tan (light brown) and dark brown. Pick one for the body of the apron and one for the straps, loops and pockets. Leather is a natural material, so tone and grain vary a little from piece to piece.

Where do you ship, and how long does it take?

Can I return a personalised apron?

Because each apron is made to order with your personalisation, we can't accept change-of-mind returns. If your order arrives faulty or isn't what we agreed in your proof, we'll put it right. See shipping, returns & refunds.

How do I pay?

Pay at our secure checkout. The available payment methods are shown at checkout. Prefer to order by message? We can take your order on WhatsApp too.

How do I look after my leather apron?

Wipe it with a damp cloth and let it dry naturally, away from direct heat. Every few months, feed the leather with a conditioner such as our leather care grease to keep it supple.

Do you have discounts or special offers?

Offers, and prices for business, team and multi-apron orders, are agreed directly with us. Message us on WhatsApp or email us and we'll talk it through.

Of course. Message us on WhatsApp ({{site.whatsappDisplay}}) or email {{site.email}}.
