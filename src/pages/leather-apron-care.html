How to clean and care for a leather apron

A full-grain leather apron needs very little care, and it gets better with use. A quick wipe after work, slow drying and a little conditioner now and then will keep it going for years.

Everyday cleaning

  • Wipe, don't wash. Use a damp cloth to wipe off dust, sauce, hair or sawdust. For grease, blot it straight away with a dry cloth before wiping.
  • Dry it slowly. Hang it up at room temperature. Keep it away from radiators, fires, the grill and direct sun, which dry leather out and can make it crack.
  • Never machine wash or tumble dry a leather apron.
  • Store it hanging, somewhere dry and airy. Don't keep it folded in a plastic bag.

Vintage look or like new? You choose

For a vintage, worn-in look that develops quickly, leave the leather untreated. It will darken, crease and pick up character from every cook, cut or project.

To keep it moisturized and looking new for longer, treat it every few months with a leather grease or conditioner. Rub a thin layer in with a soft cloth, leave it for an hour, then buff off the excess.

If it gets soaked

Blot off as much water as you can, reshape it and let it dry slowly at room temperature. Once it's fully dry, give it a thin coat of grease so the leather stays soft.
