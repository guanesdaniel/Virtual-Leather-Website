Leather apron size guide by height

A leather apron should cover your chest and end just above the knee. Too short and it leaves your clothes exposed; too long and it catches on your knees every time you bend. The right length depends mostly on your height.

How to measure

  • Height is enough for length. You don't need a tape measure: tell us the wearer's height in feet and inches or in meters.
  • Weight sets the width. We use height and weight together to choose the standard or the wider cut.
  • Buying a gift? A close guess of his height works. The neck and waist straps adjust, so a few centimeters either way still fits.

Fixed sizes vs made to measure

Most leather aprons come in one size, around 70 to 80 cm long. That suits people of average height but leaves tall people with a short apron and shorter people with one that knocks their knees.

Ours are made to measure: the designer shows the size we'll cut as soon as you type the height, and you approve a design proof before we start. Read more about the leather we use and how to look after it.
