Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use virtualleather.net or order from us, why we collect it and what your rights are. We collect only what we need to make and deliver your order.

Who is responsible for your data

Virtual Leather Intl (Daniel Guanes) is the data controller. Contact: danielguanes@virtualleather.info. TODO (owner): add the business address, and confirm the country where the business is established. This decides which data protection law applies (for example UK GDPR).

We don't sell your personal data. We don't send marketing emails unless you've asked us to, and every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link.

Who we share it with

  • Lightspeed eCom (Ecwid): our online shop, cart and checkout platform.
  • Payment providers (such as Stripe or PayPal, depending on the method you choose at checkout): to take payment and prevent fraud.
  • Our workshop in Colombia: to make your item (your personalisation, sizing and delivery details).
  • Delivery carriers: to deliver your order and provide tracking.
  • Meta (WhatsApp, Instagram): if you contact us through those apps.
  • Google (Analytics) and Meta (Pixel): only if you allow analytics or marketing cookies.
  • Hostinger: our website host, which keeps standard server logs for security.

International transfers

Our products are made in Colombia, and some of our service providers are based in the USA and other countries. Where we transfer personal data outside the UK or EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards. These include adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses used by our providers, or the transfer being necessary to perform your contract.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as the law requires for tax and accounting. TODO (owner): confirm the period, for example 6 years in the UK. We delete sizing details, logos and design files when they are no longer needed for your order or any repeat order you ask for. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • access your data or receive a copy of it;
  • correct it or delete it;
  • restrict or object to how we use it.

You can withdraw consent at any time, using for cookies. To use your rights, email danielguanes@virtualleather.info. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to your data protection authority. In the UK, that's the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
