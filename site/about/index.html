About us

A place for leather lovers and their ideas.

Virtual Leather was born from a wish to create functional, high-quality products that can be used for business and pleasure alike.

We love bringing customers' ideas to life, with real attention to detail. From choosing our smooth leather, hand-picked by our team, through to the personalisation, we're directly involved at every step.

Our aprons are now worn by business owners and home cooks from Dubai to New Zealand, and many of them come back to us for their next piece.

We're here to bring your design to life. Before production starts, we make sure you're happy with your custom design.

Daniel Guanes, founder
