Accessibility

Last updated: 2 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use our website and order from us. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we've done

  • The whole site, including the apron designer, works with a keyboard alone. Focus is always visible.
  • Text and buttons meet WCAG AA colour contrast.
  • Every form field has a visible label. Errors are explained in words, not just colour.
  • Images have text alternatives, and the designer preview is described in text.
  • Pages use headings and landmarks, so screen reader users can move around easily.
  • We respect your device's "reduce motion" setting.

Known limitations

Our cart and checkout are provided by Lightspeed eCom (Ecwid). We don't control their accessibility. If anything stops you ordering, we'll take your order directly.
